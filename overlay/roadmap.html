Roadmap

Remote access, re-signing and source-based automatic updates.

Phase 1 · Remote connectivity

Technical feasibility gate. Validate private dashboard access over cellular and determine whether Apple’s paired-device installation channel can operate across a VPN without a companion app.

  • Configure Tailscale on Mac and iPhone
  • Trace discovery, pairing, lockdown and installation
  • Test direct targeting without Bonjour
  • Document a go/no-go architecture

Phase 2 · Remote dashboard

Mobile-first PWA with paired phones, installed apps, exact signing expiration, readiness, history, diagnostics and Re-sign All. App expiry and rolling App ID limits remain separate.

Phase 3 · Remote re-signing

Persistent jobs that survive browser closure, authenticated web and Shortcuts triggers, web-based Apple verification, serialized installations and safe failure handling.

Phase 4 · Automated refresh

  • Done: schedule installed apps below six hours
  • Done: process app/device pairs sequentially
  • Done: never reinstall a removed app automatically
  • Queue while unavailable, resume when reachable and notify on failure

Phase 5 · Automatic updates

Poll configured sources, compare versions, download IPAs, apply per-app approval policy, sign/install approved updates and retain an audit history.

Android connectivity

  • USB-connected phones can switch to Wi-Fi automatically without finding pairing and connect ports
  • Keep manual Android 11 Wireless Debugging pairing as a fallback
  • Restore remembered Wi-Fi connections after restarts

macOS menu bar

  • Initial native menu-bar target and local health status
  • Signed app packaging and Launch at Login
  • Phone, expiration and job status with notifications

Constraints

  • No additional sideloaded companion app
  • Private VPN; no mandatory public exposure
  • Preserve USB and local Wi-Fi
  • Never assume Bonjour traverses VPN
  • Protect credentials, pairing records and signing material
